THE MODEL RULE REQUIRES

ORIGINAL FICTIONAL CONTRACT

In this fictional contract, YES requires Company A's shares to begin regular trading on a public exchange before Company B's shares do, and before the stated deadline. A confidential filing, public filing or announcement of intent does not count as the first trading day.

This rule was authored for the Jev Rules Lab. It is not copied from an exchange and cannot determine the outcome of a real market.
