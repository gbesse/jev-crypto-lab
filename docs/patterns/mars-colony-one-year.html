THE HEADLINE

THE MODEL RULE REQUIRES

Ten residents, a full Earth year and no essential resupply must coincide before the cutoff.

Watch the gap

A landing, short stay or regularly resupplied base is not the specified one-year colony.

03 / COMPLETE MODEL WORDING

The exact sample rule.

ORIGINAL FICTIONAL CONTRACT

In this fictional contract, YES requires at least ten people to reside continuously in a self-sufficient settlement on Mars for one full Earth year, with that year completed before the deadline. Self-sufficiency means no essential food, air or water shipments from Earth during that year.

This rule was authored for the Jev Rules Lab. It is not copied from an exchange and cannot determine the outcome of a real market.
